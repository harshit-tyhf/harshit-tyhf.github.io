Be a god, and a fearsome one at that. You don't want the people to take you for granted. Make them feel your presence.  
Manage the weather, rain and sunshine affect the yield meter, which in-turn affects the population of the village. You can't kill them all, or else who would be left to fear you ?

# How it came to be
Me and my friends (Aarav Mittal and Shreyansh Saras) were participating as a team in a 24 hour game jam hosted by our college during our tech fest (Tech Solstice '26). The most challenging part was to make everything work together. I still remember the music and sounds (especially the win music) were a pain to set-up especially since we were all <span style="white-space: nowrap">sleep-deprived</span> at that time. But I am proud of what we managed to achieve in 24 hours from scratch.
